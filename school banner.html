About Our School

Learning, Growing, and Achieving Together

Francisco G. Nepomuceno Memorial High School is more than just a place of learning. It is a community where students are inspired to excel, discover their talents, and create unforgettable experiences. We provide quality education while offering engaging activities such as Recognition Day, Content-Based Integration (CBI), Career Immersion, Special Needs Education (SNED) Program, Intramurals, Buwan ng Wika Celebration, Cultural Dance and Theater Performances, Science and Technology Exhibits, Academic Competitions, Leadership Training, Clubs and Organizations, Community Outreach Programs, and various Senior High School events. These activities help students develop their knowledge, skills, creativity, leadership, teamwork, and confidence, preparing them to become responsible and successful individuals.

MEMORIES AND EXPERIENCES

Our School Activities

Celebrating creativity, teamwork, talents, and achievements

01

Grade 11 Contemporary Dance: Against Corruption

Grade 11 students presented a contemporary dance performance that raised awareness about the effects of corruption and encouraged honesty, integrity, and social responsibility through expressive movement.

02

Grade 12 Festival Competition

Students showcased their talents through vibrant festival-inspired performances, celebrating Filipino culture, traditions, and teamwork through music and dance.

03

Zumba Fitness Activity

Students and teachers participated in an energetic Zumba session to promote physical fitness, healthy living, and an active lifestyle while having fun together.

04

Career Outfit Showcase

Students wore career-inspired outfits to represent different professions, helping them explore future career opportunities while developing confidence, creativity, and communication skills.

05

Special Needs Education (SNED) Activity

This educational activity challenged students to think and respond quickly through interactive learning tasks that improved their knowledge, critical thinking, and teamwork.

06

Science Creation Seminar

The activity introduced the "Book of the World" to students, encouraging them to discover new knowledge about biology, appreciate global cultures, and develop a love for science, reading, and learning.

07

Contemporary Dance Performance by the SNED

The SNED students performed a contemporary dance that expressed emotions and meaningful messages through graceful movements, showcasing their creativity and artistic talents.

08

CookFest Event for Grade 12 BE Students

BE students showcased their culinary and entrepreneurial skills by preparing and selling a variety of delicious food. The event promoted teamwork, creativity, customer service, and basic business skills while giving students hands-on experience in food entrepreneurship.

09

Sabayang Pagbigkas for Buwan ng Wika

Students performed a synchronized choral recitation that highlighted the beauty of the Filipino language while promoting unity, patriotism, and appreciation of Filipino culture.

10

Buwan ng Wika Hat Design Competition with Fashion Model

Students designed creative hats inspired by Filipino culture and traditions, then confidently modeled their creations to showcase their artistic skills and cultural pride.
